Question 1:
Pros of Agile development: It is an iterative process to software development, it lets you break down the project into small stages, and it allows for constant value add/feedback to/from the customer.
Cons of Agile development: It requires more careful planning to break down the project into stages, it puts more pressure on engineers to deliver results at the end of each sprint, and it could require more infrastructure to support Continuous Integration.


Question 2:
Scrum Master: The Scrum Master handles all scrum related tasks, including working with JIRA and managing all the sprints and team meetings.
Product Owner: The Product Owner has broader responsibilities, including setting the direction for the team, handling releases, and managing stakeholders.


Question 3:
Spring Planning Meeting: In this meeting, the team determines what to complete in the coming sprint, moves issues from the backlog into the current sprint, creates story points and assigns stories to the team, and finally starts the sprint.
Daily Scrum/Standup Meeting: In this short 10-minute meeting every day during the sprint, the team members discuss the work completed, the plan for the day, and any blockers.
Sprint Retrospective Meeting: This meeting occurs at the end of the sprint and the team discusses the work completed and moves any incomplete work back into the backlog and finally closes the sprint.
